# Privacy Policy

URL Prefix Blocker does not collect, sell, or send any personal data.

## What the extension reads

- The URL of the page a tab is opening. It compares the URL with your block list, inside your browser, and then forgets it. URLs are never saved or sent anywhere.

## What the extension stores

- Your block list (the prefixes you type in the popup). It is saved with `chrome.storage.sync`.
  If you are signed in to Chrome with sync on, Chrome syncs this list to your other computers through your Google account. The developer cannot see it.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- No network requests to any server.
- It never reads the content of web pages.
